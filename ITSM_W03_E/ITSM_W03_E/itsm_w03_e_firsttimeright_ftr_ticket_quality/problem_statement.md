# ITSM Lab: First-Time-Right (FTR) & Ticket Quality Audit - Resolving Tier-Bouncing & Incomplete Work Notes

Duration : 60 Min.

## Scenario
A service delivery manager notices that team FTR has fallen to 58%, primarily caused by premature ticket escalation and vague closure notes. You must audit 10 historical tickets, identify instances of tier-bouncing, rewrite three poorly closed tickets against LTM quality standards, and provide actionable recommendations to elevate FTR above the 85% target threshold.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Calculate historical FTR percentage from sample incident dataset and identify tier-bouncing patterns.
- Audit 10 ticket work note entries to identify premature escalation anti-patterns.
- Rewrite substandard closure notes into compliant LTM Close Notes with verified technical verification details.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Accurate calculation of FTR metric and identification of tier-bouncing incident records: 4 marks
2. Audit of work notes and critique of premature or context-lacking escalations: 3 marks
3. Drafting of compliant, high-quality Close Notes satisfying audit standards to drive FTR improvement: 3 marks

**Total Score: 10 Marks**
