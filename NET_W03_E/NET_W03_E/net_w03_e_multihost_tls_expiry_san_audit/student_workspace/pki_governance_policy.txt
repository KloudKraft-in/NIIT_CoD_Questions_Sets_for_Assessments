Enterprise PKI Policy Standards:
1. Alert Thresholds: Warning at 30 days remaining; Critical Escalation at 14 days remaining.
2. Expired certificates (days_remaining < 0) require immediate emergency replacement.
3. Every certificate must contain the exact connecting FQDN in the X509v3 Subject Alternative Name (SAN) extension.
4. Deprecate single-hostname certificates in favor of multi-SAN or wildcard where applicable.
