# ITSM Lab: Enterprise Change Advisory Board (CAB) Governance, Emergency RFC & Rollback Simulation

Duration : 90 Min.

## Scenario
A major version upgrade to the core container platform is proposed alongside an urgent zero-day vulnerability patch. Act as the Change Manager: review three RFC proposals (Standard, Normal, Emergency), evaluate risk assessment matrices and test evidence, interrogate rollback strategies, conduct a simulated CAB review session, and manage emergency change authorization following a failed deployment.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Evaluate change proposals against ITIL change governance criteria.
- Conduct comprehensive risk and impact assessments for a Normal production release.
- Audit rollback plans for feasibility, automated triggers, and execution time limits.
- Facilitate a simulated CAB meeting with multi-stakeholder consensus.
- Process an Emergency Change Request (eCAB) under active incident conditions.
- Execute change post-implementation review (PIR) identifying reasons for change failure.
- Update Standard Change catalogue items to improve deployment velocity.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Rigorous categorization and governance review of Standard, Normal, and Emergency RFC proposals: 3 marks
2. Comprehensive risk assessment scoring evaluating blast radius, concurrency, and dependencies: 3 marks
3. Technical validation of rollback plans including automated abort criteria and RTO alignment: 3 marks
4. Facilitation and documentation of formal CAB review with stakeholder sign-offs and scheduling: 3 marks
5. Management of Emergency Change (eCAB) workflow with compressed approval and post-fix audit: 3 marks
6. Execution of Change Post-Implementation Review (PIR) identifying deployment failure factors: 3 marks
7. Curation and promotion of proven operational procedures into the pre-approved Standard Change catalogue: 2 marks

**Total Score: 20 Marks**
