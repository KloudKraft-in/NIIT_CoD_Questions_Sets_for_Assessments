# ITSM Lab: Enterprise Service Operations Transformation - SVS, Escalation Governance & KPI Optimization

Duration : 90 Min.

## Scenario
An enterprise operating under legacy siloed operations suffers from chronic SLA breaches (SLA compliance 68%, MTTR 8.5 hours, FTR 52%). As the Lead ITSM Transformation Architect, design a complete overhaul of service operations: realign the operating model to the ITIL 4 Service Value System, redesign the iRUN support tower escalation hierarchy, implement unified observability and automation tooling workflows, re-architect the service catalogue, and establish an SLA/KPI governance framework targeting >95% SLA compliance.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Perform comprehensive gap analysis of current legacy operations against ITIL 4 standards.
- Re-architect the iRUN support tower roles (L1.5, L2, L3, SRE, AIOps) and eliminate tier-bouncing.
- Establish clear functional and hierarchical escalation matrices with strict context requirements.
- Design an integrated tooling architecture (ServiceNow, Datadog, Ansible, Confluence).
- Re-structure the Service Request Catalogue with automated approvals and SLAs.
- Formulate a continuous improvement framework based on 5-Whys and blameless PIRs.
- Model projected KPI improvements across MTTA, MTTR, FTR, and SLA compliance.
- Deliver an executive transformation roadmap with milestone phases and governance checkpoints.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
