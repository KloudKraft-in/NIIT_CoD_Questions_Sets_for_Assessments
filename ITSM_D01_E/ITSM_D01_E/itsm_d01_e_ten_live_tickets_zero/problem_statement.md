# ITSM Lab: Ten Live Tickets, Zero Mistakes - Run-Tower Role Mapping & ITIL Process Routing

Duration : 30 Min.

## Scenario
A newly onboarded service desk engineer is provided with an iRUN estate organizational hierarchy and 10 redacted operational tickets from the previous week. The engineer must inspect each ticket description, determine the correct governing ITIL 4 process (Incident, Problem, Change, Service Request, or SLM), route the ticket to the appropriate support tier (L1.5, L2, L3, SRE, or AIOps), and convert a legacy siloed escalation flow into an integrated iRUN flow with projected MTTR delta.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Analyze 10 incoming ticket payloads and accurately map each to its governing ITIL 4 lifecycle practice.
- Assign each ticket to the exact iRUN support role with technical justification.
- Draft an integrated iRUN escalation workflow replacing legacy tier-bouncing, estimating the MTTR reduction.
