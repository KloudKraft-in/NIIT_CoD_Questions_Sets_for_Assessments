# ITSM Lab: Service Request Lifecycle Management - Complex Access & Asset Provisioning Catalogue

Duration : 90 Min.

## Scenario
An organizational restructuring requires fulfilling 10 complex service requests involving software licensing, cloud IAM roles, elevated database privileges, and hardware allocation. Coordinate multi-tier approval chains, validate licensing prerequisites, track fulfillment SLAs, and execute compliant closure.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Map 10 service requests to correct catalogue items (RITM) and task breakdowns (SCTASK).
- Manage multi-level manager and security approvals.
- Resolve provisioning roadblocks without breaching Fulfilment SLAs.
- Audit closure documentation and asset tracking updates.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Correct decomposition of service requests into RITM items and sequential child SCTASKs: 3 marks
2. Verification and enforcement of multi-stage approval workflows and security authorizations: 3 marks
3. Monitoring and adherence to catalogue Fulfilment SLAs under volume conditions: 2 marks
4. Compliant closure of request records with asset management and license tracking updates: 2 marks

**Total Score: 10 Marks**
