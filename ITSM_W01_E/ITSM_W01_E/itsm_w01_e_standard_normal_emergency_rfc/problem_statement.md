# ITSM Lab: Standard, Normal & Emergency RFC Authoring - Change Advisory Board (CAB) Preparation

Duration : 60 Min.

## Scenario
A critical database parameter modification and an urgent security patch must be scheduled for deployment across production clusters. You must distinguish between Standard, Normal, and Emergency change workflows, author an audit-ready Request for Change (RFC) including risk assessment and comprehensive rollback plan, and prepare submission materials for Change Advisory Board (CAB) review.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Classify proposed changes into Standard, Normal, and Emergency categories with clear governance justification.
- Author a complete Request for Change (RFC) documenting change scope, technical justification, and testing evidence.
- Formulate a verified rollback plan detailing trigger criteria, backout steps, and maximum time to reverse.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Accurate classification of change proposals into Standard, Normal, and Emergency categories: 4 marks
2. Complete RFC authoring with mandatory technical sections (change scope, risk assessment, test evidence): 3 marks
3. Formulation of comprehensive, step-by-step rollback/backout plan and scheduling constraints: 3 marks

**Total Score: 10 Marks**
