# ITSM Lab: Eight Vague Tickets Land in One Hour - ServiceNow CMDB CI Lookup & Impact/Urgency Matrix

Duration : 30 Min.

## Scenario
A batch of eight loosely described, vague user tickets arrives at the Service Desk within a 60-minute window. Several tickets describe overlapping symptoms across database, frontend, and corporate VPN tiers. The analyst must interrogate the ServiceNow CMDB to identify the true impacted Configuration Item (CI), evaluate business impact and user urgency, assign the correct ITIL Priority (P1-P4), eliminate duplicate tickets, and route each cleanly without bounce-backs.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Perform Configuration Item (CI) lookup in the CMDB for 8 vague incident descriptions.
- Evaluate Impact (Enterprise/Department/Single User) and Urgency (Critical/High/Medium/Low) to compute the priority matrix score.
- Identify and merge duplicate ticket reports into a single parent incident, establishing clean routing.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Correct CMDB Configuration Item (CI) resolution and identification for all 8 tickets: 4 marks
2. Accurate calculation and application of the Impact x Urgency priority matrix (P1 to P4): 3 marks
3. Identification of duplicate/correlated incident reports and proper linking to parent record: 3 marks

**Total Score: 10 Marks**
