# ITSM Lab: ITIL v4 Service Value Chain Traceability - Mapping Incident Lifecycle Through Six Activities

Duration : 60 Min.

## Scenario
An enterprise e-commerce platform experiences repeated service degradation during marketing campaigns. The service operations team must trace the lifecycle of this recurring incident across all six Service Value Chain activities (Plan, Improve, Engage, Design & Transition, Obtain/Build, Deliver & Support) and demonstrate how the ITIL 4 Guiding Principles (such as 'Focus on Value' and 'Collaborate and Promote Visibility') guide tactical decision-making.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Map the complete end-to-end lifecycle of an incident through all six Service Value Chain activities.
- Demonstrate that the Service Value Chain is an interconnected network rather than a linear waterfall process.
- Apply three ITIL 4 Guiding Principles to real service desk operational challenges.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.

4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

1. Complete and accurate mapping of the incident lifecycle across all six Service Value Chain activities: 4 marks
2. Demonstration of non-linear activity triggers (how Deliver & Support triggers Improve and Design & Transition): 3 marks
3. Concrete application of at least three ITIL 4 Guiding Principles to resolve operational trade-offs: 3 marks
