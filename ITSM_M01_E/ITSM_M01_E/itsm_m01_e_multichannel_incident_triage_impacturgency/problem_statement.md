# ITSM Lab: Multi-Channel Incident Triage & Impact/Urgency Prioritization Across Enterprise Services

Duration : 90 Min.

## Scenario
A storm of 15 incoming alerts and user tickets arrives across email, portal, chat, and monitoring webhooks following an infrastructure maintenance window. Perform deduplication, CMDB CI resolution, Impact x Urgency matrix calculation, and route to L1.5/L2 resolver groups within SLA countdown limits.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Ingest and normalize 15 multi-channel support tickets.
- Resolve CI dependencies across web, app, and database tiers.
- Assign P1-P4 priorities using business criticality matrices.
- Eliminate duplicate incidents and author initial triage notes.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Accurate multi-channel ingestion, normalization, and deduplication of ticket records: 3 marks
2. CMDB CI dependency mapping across multi-tier architecture: 3 marks
3. Mathematical Impact x Urgency priority calculations and SLA target assignment: 2 marks
4. Generation of structured initial triage work notes and dispatch to correct resolver groups: 2 marks

**Total Score: 10 Marks**
