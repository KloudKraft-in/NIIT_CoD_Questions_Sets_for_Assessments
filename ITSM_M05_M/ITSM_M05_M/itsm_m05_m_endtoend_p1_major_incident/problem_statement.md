# ITSM Lab: End-to-End P1 Major Incident Management, Bridge Coordination & SLA Breach Remediation

Duration : 90 Min.

## Scenario
A catastrophic failure in the core customer authentication service triggers hundreds of failed logins, impacting $2M/hour in transactions. Mobilize and coordinate a P1 Major Incident from initial detection through bridge call coordination, executive communications, multi-team technical triage, service restoration, and SLA reconciliation.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Mobilize P1 Major Incident command and establish bridge hierarchy.
- Scribe live decision log and hypothesis testing records.
- Deliver crisp technical contributor updates.
- Issue T+15 and T+30 executive notifications.
- Coordinate cross-functional triage across Identity, Network, and Cloud teams.
- Execute emergency change or rollback to restore service.
- Calculate MTTA/MTTR and evaluate SLA compliance.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Immediate declaration of P1 Major Incident with correct CMDB CI linkage and business impact tagging: 3 marks
2. Mobilization and management of bridge call command structure with clear Scribe and Incident Commander roles: 3 marks
3. Maintenance of chronological decision and action log capturing all diagnostic hypotheses: 3 marks
4. Authoring and broadcast of timely, compliant executive stakeholder updates at T+15 and T+30 minutes: 3 marks
5. Coordination of multi-team technical triage without cross-talk or communication bottlenecks: 3 marks
6. Successful execution of emergency service restoration with validated verification criteria: 3 marks
7. Accurate computation of MTTA, MTTR, and SLA breach analysis with pause-time audit: 2 marks

**Total Score: 20 Marks**
