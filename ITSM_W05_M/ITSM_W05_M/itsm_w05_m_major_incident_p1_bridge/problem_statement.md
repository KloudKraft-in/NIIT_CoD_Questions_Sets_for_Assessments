# ITSM Lab: Major Incident P1 Bridge Call Participation - Scribe Discipline & Technical Contributor Etiquette

Duration : 60 Min.

## Scenario
A critical P1 payment gateway outage halts transaction processing. A high-priority technical bridge call is mobilized involving Network, Database, Platform, and Payment Vendor teams. Trainees must participate in the live bridge command structure: first performing the Scribe role (recording real-time chronological timestamps, key hypothesis tests, and decision ownership logs), then rotating to Technical Contributor (providing crisp, 30-second situational status reports on database connectivity without talking over other streams), and producing a T+15/T+30 stakeholder update.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Maintain an active Major Incident Decision & Action Log as bridge Scribe.
- Deliver structured, concise status updates adhering to bridge etiquette as Technical Contributor.
- Draft an executive stakeholder update at T+30 minutes detailing business impact, current hypothesis, and Estimated Time to Restore (ETR).
- Synchronize ServiceNow Major Incident record fields with live bridge actions.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.
