# ITSM Lab: Cross-Domain Functional Escalation & Handover Audit - Resolving Complex Database Lockups

Duration : 90 Min.

## Scenario
An unresolved database latency issue is passed back and forth between Application, Database, and Infrastructure teams due to poor escalation notes. Apply iRUN escalation standards to audit the ticket history, identify escalation anti-patterns ('throwing over the wall', missing diagnostic logs), build a comprehensive diagnostic handover package, and transfer the ticket to the L3 Database Engineering resolver group.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Audit historical escalation logs to identify communication breakdown points.
- Apply functional escalation criteria according to the iRUN matrix.
- Compile a zero-bounce diagnostic package (lock statistics, query plans, CPU/memory profiles).
- Draft a compliant handover communication to L3 engineering.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.

## Evaluation Rubric:
1. Critical audit of ticket history identifying root causes of inter-tier bouncing: 3 marks
2. Application of iRUN matrix rules to establish correct resolver group ownership: 3 marks
3. Compilation of exhaustive diagnostic context package preventing ticket return: 2 marks
4. Authoring of formal escalation handover note adhering to zero-bounce standards: 2 marks

**Total Score: 10 Marks**
