# ITSM Lab: Comprehensive Post-Incident Review, 5-Whys Analysis & Blameless SRE Remediation

Duration : 90 Min.

## Scenario
Following a severe 3-hour core banking ledger outage, the organization requires an exhaustive, audit-grade Post-Incident Review (PIR). Conduct an end-to-end retrospective: reconstruct the complete chronological timeline from telemetry and bridge notes, execute a multi-branch 5-Whys root cause analysis, identify technical and organizational contributing factors, and author a formal LTM PIR report with SMART corrective and preventive actions.

## Task Objectives
Perform the following actions inside the `student_workspace` directory:
- Reconstruct a minute-by-minute timeline across detection, mobilization, triage, fix, and verification.
- Execute deep 5-Whys root cause analysis uncovering underlying systemic flaws.
- Evaluate contributing factors across monitoring visibility, testing gaps, and runbook accuracy.
- Formulate SMART preventive actions with assigned engineering owners and target delivery dates.
- Calculate SLA impact, downtime cost, and error budget depletion.
- Prepare executive summary for C-level leadership.
- Update Knowledge Base and Known Error Database with lessons learned.

## Instructions to Perform the Task
1. When your workspace loads in **VS Code**, use the **Explorer** panel on the left to locate your files.
2. Navigate to and click the specific files mentioned in the Task Objectives.
3. Make the necessary code edits in the editor.
4. Press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) or `Cmd + S` (Mac) to save your changes.
5. If you need to run commands, open the built-in terminal by clicking **Terminal > New Terminal** from the top menu.

## Validation
Once you have saved your files and are ready to submit, return to the platform dashboard and click the **"Submit Final"** button. This task is evaluated manually by the grading team.
